Why CourseLens

Studying from scattered files is harder than it should be

The problem

  • Course material lives in scattered PDFs, slide decks, scans, and text files.
  • Finding one specific concept means skimming page after page.
  • Keyword search misses context — it matches words, not meaning.
  • Generic chatbots answer from the internet, not from your material.

How CourseLens addresses it

  • Upload your material once — PDFs, slides, text, Markdown, and images stay in one place.
  • Every page and slide is extracted, chunked, and indexed for meaning, not exact wording.
  • Semantic retrieval finds the passages that actually answer your question.
  • Answers are generated only from retrieved evidence, each with a page-level source you can open.

How it works

From raw files to grounded answers

  1. 1

    Upload your material

    Add lecture PDFs, slide decks, text notes, Markdown, or scanned images.

  2. 2

    CourseLens processes and indexes it

    Each page is extracted, chunked, and embedded into a vector index for meaning-based retrieval.

  3. 3

    Ask questions or search

    Ask in your own words — retrieval finds the relevant passages across everything you uploaded.

  4. 4

    Learn from contextual answers

    Get an answer tied to the exact page or slide it came from, and open the source directly.

Technology

An honest, modern stack

Angular 19 + TypeScript

Standalone-component frontend with a fast, typed single-page interface.

Spring Boot

Java 17 backend exposing a small REST API with Spring MVC.

PostgreSQL + pgvector

Vector(768) embeddings stored natively, queried with cosine distance over an HNSW index.

Vector embeddings

Pages and questions are embedded for semantic similarity, not keyword matching.

Flyway migrations

Schema and indexes are versioned and reproducible from day one.

AI / LLM integration

Gemini powers embeddings, scanned-page vision transcription, and grounded answer generation.

About

Early stage, openly built

CourseLens is an early-stage product under active development. It exists for one reason: studying from your own course material should be faster and more trustworthy than skimming files or asking a general-purpose chatbot. That is why every feature — ingestion, retrieval, and generation — is designed around answers that stay grounded in your uploads and can be traced back to the exact page they came from.

The product is evolving quickly, and the source is available on GitHub. No user counts, metrics, or endorsements are claimed on this page — just the product, as it is today.
